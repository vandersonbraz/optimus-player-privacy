Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Optimus Player is a media player application for Samsung Smart TV. It lets you load and watch your own media playlists on your television. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

In short: Optimus Player does not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, payment details or location. It only uses your TV device identifiers to deliver the playlist that you configured for your device, and it does not sell or share your data.

Information the app uses

  • Device identifiers (MAC address and Device ID). The app reads your television's MAC address and a device ID and sends them to our server only to locate and load the media playlist that is associated with your device.
  • Playlist source. The address of the media playlist you provide, so the app can load your content.
  • Favorites and settings. Items you mark as favorite and your preferences (such as language) are stored locally on your television.

Information we do NOT collect

  • Name, username, date of birth, age or gender
  • E-mail address, phone number or postal address
  • Payment or financial data
  • Precise location, voice recordings, photos or biometric data
  • Web browsing history

How we use the information

Device identifiers are used for a single purpose: to match your television with the correct media playlist that was configured for it, so the app can load your content. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not track your behavior across other services.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Device identifiers are processed only through our own secure backend (Google Firebase) for the purpose described above.

Content disclaimer

Optimus Player does not include or provide any media content. All content is added by the user through their own playlists. The app is a neutral media player and is not responsible for the content you choose to use with it.

Data retention

Device identifiers are kept only while needed to associate your device with its playlist. Favorites and settings remain on your device and are removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

The app itself contains no mature content and includes a Parental Control feature. It is suitable for a general audience.
